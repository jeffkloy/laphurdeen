# The Ministries of the Commonwealth of Laphurdeen

Article 24 of the Constitution establishes twelve Ministries, and they are
the whole of the executive: Laphurdeen has no President and no Prime
Minister. Executive authority vests in the Senate acting collectively as
the Council of the Commonwealth (Article 26), and every Senator is a
**Lead Minister**, elected by national popular vote to a named seat within
a Ministry (Article 18). The Lead Ministers of a Ministry together form
its **Ministerial College**, direct the Ministry collegially, and divide
its portfolios among themselves. A candidate for a Ministry's seat must
satisfy the professional and integrity qualifications fixed for that
Ministry by the Electoral and Qualifications Commission - the health seats
are contested by people qualified in health, the finance seats by people
qualified in finance. Beneath the Colleges works a professional,
merit-based, politically impartial civil service (Article 25).

Ministries may be created, merged, renamed, or dissolved only by Act of
Parliament under the Consensus Rule, taking effect from the next general
election (Article 24(2)). In Laphurdi the twelve are the *Ministerieren*;
each entry below carries the Laphurdi name as the Grundlojen writes it.

## Ministry of Justice and Rights

*Ministeriet for Justis og Rekter*

The keeper of the Charter of Rights and Freedoms inside the executive. It
drafts the Commonwealth's laws, runs legal aid so that counsel at public
expense reaches everyone who cannot afford it (Article 15), and operates a
corrections service whose constitutional aim is rehabilitation - in a
country that has abolished the death penalty forever and prohibits torture
absolutely. Its registry offices perform the simple administrative acts
the Charter promises: marriage and civil union open to any two consenting
adults, and legal recognition of gender identity by self-determination
(Article 6). It supports the courts with buildings and budgets but never
with instructions; judicial independence belongs to Part VI.

## Ministry of Public Safety and Integrity

*Ministeriet for Folksekerhed og Integritet*

Police, fire, and emergency services, under the Charter's hard limits:
mass indiscriminate surveillance is prohibited, and targeted surveillance
moves only on prior judicial authorisation (Article 9). The police do
not carry firearms - only a special operations unit designated by law
may bear them - and every bullet is accounted for: a person shot by an
agent of the Commonwealth is owed a public report stating, for each
bullet, why it was fired into each specific organ it struck
(Article 15). The integrity
half of its name is the machinery of clean public life under Article 33 -
the public registers of interests, assets, gifts, and lobbying contacts,
and the publication of contracts, budgets, and expenditure in open,
machine-readable form. The watchdogs themselves - the Integrity
Commission, the Auditor-General, and the Public Advocate - are
independent constitutional bodies (Articles 32 and 34); this Ministry
resources them and answers their findings, and may never direct them.

## Ministry of Foreign Affairs and Trade

*Ministeriet for Utland og Handel*

The Commonwealth's voice abroad. It negotiates treaties on the authority
of the Council - binding only upon ratification by both chambers under
the Consensus Rule (Article 28) - maintains the embassies and consulates,
and manages trade policy for an island nation that lives by its ports. It
prepares the ceremonial diplomacy of the Speaker of the Commons, who
accredits and receives ambassadors as head of state (Article 27), and it
carries Laphurdeen's commitments to international benchmarks - health,
transport, transparency - back home for publication.

## Ministry of Finance and the Treasury

*Ministeriet for Finans og Skatt*

The Laphurdi name says it plainly: finance and tax. Its Ministerial
College proposes the annual budget (Article 36), and if Parliament does
not pass one, the previous budget continues in force - the operations of
the Commonwealth never cease for want of an appropriation. It administers
the constitutional taxes of Article 35: the flat income tax above the
million-kronur threshold, the flat profits tax, the sales tax that lives
inside every displayed price, and the wealth tax on net wealth above one
hundred million kronur. Under Article 35(8) it runs the assessment in
*santid* - true time - calculating and collecting as income arrives and
sales are made, so that no person is required to file a tax return. It
also carries the household-debt duties of Article 37: capping the cost of
consumer credit, guaranteeing free financial counselling, and publishing
the debt-to-income statistics every year. The Reserve Bank stands beside
it, not beneath it (Article 38).

## Ministry of Health

*Helsaministeriet*

Universal healthcare, comprehensive in scope and free at the point of use
(Article 10) - including mental healthcare, reproductive healthcare, and
gender-affirming care, none of which may be denied by reason of cost. The
Ministry must keep standards among the best in the world and publish the
proof annually against international benchmarks. It regulates the lawful
production and sale of soft drugs, and treats drug dependence as what the
Constitution says it is: a matter of health, not of punishment.

## Ministry of Education and Research

*Ministeriet for Lering og Forsking*

Free, universal early childhood, primary, and secondary education, and
the constitutional ceiling on tertiary tuition - never more than four per
cent of the national median annual income per academic year, with no
student forced to borrow commercially to meet it (Article 11). It teaches
in both official languages, Laphurdi and English (Article 4), funds the
universities and research institutes, and guards a line it may not cross:
academic freedom and institutional autonomy are guaranteed.

## Ministry of Social Welfare and Housing

*Ministeriet for Velvard og Hem*

The administrator of the **Laphurdeen Guarantee** (Article 13), the
Commonwealth's enforceable duty of care to its least-advantaged quarter
and to every person with a disability, the quarter identified by a
transparent statutory index of income, wealth, and circumstance and the
disabled within the Guarantee as of right. Through it flow the Guarantee's minimum
entitlements: an income floor sufficient for a dignified life; secure and
adequate housing; energy, water, and digital connectivity; and priority
access to education, training, and employment services. Its funding is a
first charge on the revenues of the Commonwealth, after the service of
public debt - the budget line that stands in front of every other.

## Ministry of Labour and Economic Development

*Ministeriet for Werk og Ekonomi*

The guardian of Article 12: the living wage fixed by law and reviewed
annually, limits on working hours, safe workplaces, paid annual, sick,
and parental leave, and equal pay for work of equal value. It protects
the right to organise, bargain, and strike, and the entitlement of
workers to representation in the governance of large enterprises. Sex
work between consenting adults is lawful work under its full protection.
Its development side tends the wider economy - the trades, the ports,
the small firms taxed at one flat rate - so that just conditions and
prosperity arrive together.

## Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport

*Ministeriet for Bygging og Transport*

The builder. *Bygging* is building, and this Ministry delivers the
Article 14 guarantee: a frequent, safe, affordable, fully accessible
public transport network reaching substantially the whole territory, with
fares capped by law and free travel for persons within the Laphurdeen
Guarantee. Public and active transport take priority over private motor
transport in the planning, funding, and design of public space. It is
the home of **Baaner Laphurdeen**, the national carrier, alongside the
DCTS of the Darcambrian Communities (see TRANSPORT.md), and it reports
annually on coverage, frequency, accessibility, and affordability
against the best standards in the world.

## Ministry of Environment and Marine Resources

*Ministeriet for Natur og Zee*

Nature and the sea, as the Laphurdi name has it - the trust that holds an
archipelago together. It stewards the internal waters, territorial sea,
and seabed of Article 2, manages the fisheries that fill the morning
markets from Easthaven to Agaetisboro, and keeps the cork-oak forests,
salt pans, and flamingo lagoons of the provinces. It advises Parliament
on the duties and charges directed at harm and scarcity that Article
35(7) permits, so that what damages the commons pays the commons back.

## Ministry of Culture, Media and Digital Society

*Ministeriet for Kultur, Media og Digital Werld*

The keeper of the national language and the national story. It tends
Laphurdi as the national language and both official languages in public
life (Article 4), the flag, anthem, and symbols of Article 5, and the
arts, museums, and festivals of the Commonwealth. Toward the media it
funds but never directs: the freedom and pluralism of the press are
guaranteed, and prior restraint is all but impossible (Article 7). Its
digital portfolio builds the open, machine-readable public data of
Article 33, the right of access to information held by public bodies,
and the digital connectivity the Guarantee promises - under the data
protection and anti-surveillance rules of Article 9.

## Ministry of Defence and Civil Protection

*Ministeriet for Forsvar og Folkskydd*

The defence forces of Laphurdeen stand under strict civilian control,
directed by this Ministerial College within policy set by the Council
(Article 28). No deployment of force beyond the territory, and no
declaration of a state of war, is lawful without the approval of both
chambers under the Consensus Rule. Civil protection is the other half:
disaster response, search and rescue in the waters of the archipelago,
and the machinery of the states of emergency of Article 42 - which must
be necessary, proportionate, and end-dated, and which can never close
Parliament.
