# Transport in the Commonwealth of Laphurdeen

Article 14 of the Constitution makes public transport a right: a frequent,
safe, affordable, fully accessible network reaching substantially the whole
territory, with fares capped by law and free travel for persons within the
Laphurdeen Guarantee. Two operators carry that promise: **Baaner
Laphurdeen**, the national carrier, and the **DCTS**, the metro network of
the Darcambrian Communities.

## Baaner Laphurdeen — the national carrier

*Baaner* is the heritage plural of *baan* ("track, way"), keeping its
Charter-era spelling like the ⟨ph⟩ of Laphurdeen: **"Railways
Laphurdeen."** The carrier is public, its network is the Article 14
guarantee made visible, and its amber-on-blue trains are the flag at speed.

### The express triangle

Three express corridors link the three major cities, hourly through the
day, half-hourly at the peaks:

- **Lapentieur ⇄ Darlingmoors** — capital to principal city, calling at
  Darlingmoors Central on the City Centre Mainline.
- **Darlingmoors ⇄ Agaetisboro** — the amber curve to the sunny south.
- **Lapentieur ⇄ Agaetisboro** — the capital's fog to Southcape's sun.

### The regional lines

Regional trains radiate from the triangle to every province seat and the
country towns between:

- **Eastern Line:** Lapentieur – Fernlea – Woldham – Easthaven, with a
  delta branch Woldham – Linbridge – Linmouth.
- **Southern Line:** Lapentieur – Vindalen – Saltvik – Agaetisboro.
- **Western Line:** Lapentieur – Ashcombe – Moorgate – Belhaven, with a
  forest branch Moorgate – Pineforest.
- **Northern Line:** Darlingmoors – Millford – Whitmere – Highcliffe –
  Sundby.
- **Gorge Line:** Darlingmoors – Highbridge, up the Peaklands grades on
  the Charter-era stone spans.

### The rail-ferry sailings

The island provinces are on the same ticket. Baaner Laphurdeen's
integrated rail-ferry sailings continue the timetable onto the water:

- **Belhaven ⇄ Shellharbour** — to the outer isles of Sainte-Agathe.
- **Sundby ⇄ the Straits towns** — the crossings that are the Northern
  Straits' calendar.

One ticket, boat included; the fare cap applies at sea as on land.

### Fares

- The Article 14(4) fare cap, made concrete: a **weekly cap of
  Kr. 4,950** across the whole Baaner Laphurdeen network — trains,
  branches, and rail-ferry sailings alike. Ride as much as the week
  holds; the network stops charging at the cap.
- **Tap-to-pay everywhere:** contactless credit and debit cards, and
  Apple Pay and Google Pay, at every gate and gangway. The cap is applied
  automatically to whatever was tapped — no ticket office, no
  registration, no paper.
- Persons within the Laphurdeen Guarantee travel free, per Article 14(4).
- Prices are tax-inclusive, as all prices in Laphurdeen are.

## DCTS — the Darcambrian Communities Transport Service

The metro network of Darcambria province, run jointly by the eight Charter
cities as the Darcambrian Communities; Darcambrians call the whole system
**"the T."** National trains meet it at the **City Centre Mainline**
stations on Darlingmoors: **Central, Market Square, Old Courthouse**.

### The dialling plan (and the zones it names)

Telephone dialling plan 0221, whose codes double as the city's shorthand
for its own geography:

| Zone | Code | Districts |
|---|---|---|
| City Centre | 0221 2 | City Centre |
| C | 0221 3 | Outer Docks, Government Centre, Inner Docks, Yacht Harbour |
| NW | 0221 4 | Seaport Village, The Northwest, Outlook, Darling Hill, Timberland |
| NE | 0221 5 | Oyster Point, Forest Park, The Beaches |
| SW | 0221 6 | Peak District, Eastern Peak, Inner Gorge, Outer Gorge |
| SE | 0221 7 | Southeast Slopes, Shoreline Rocks, Cambrian Junction |

Suburban zones: SW, Winchester-on-the-Sea to Riverlin; SE, Linear Village
to Briarside South.

### DCTS fares

Capped like everything under Article 14, tap-to-pay like everything on
Baaner Laphurdeen. One exception rides above the cap: journeys to and
from the Airport carry a premium of **Kr. 320**.
