Practical Laphurdeen

The price is the price, the trains run often, and a little Laphurdi opens many doors. This is the practical side of the country with three words on every coin.

The krona and the price tag

The krona stands fast

Article 38 fixes the krona; Article 35(4) puts the tax inside the price. You need exactly one rule: divide by a hundred.

Kr. 100 = € 1 - always

The krona is pegged at one hundred kronur to the euro (Art. 38) - that's the Constitution, not the market. A Kr. 450 ticket is €4.50; a Kr. 4,950 week on the rails is €49.50.

The price you see is the price you pay

Sales tax lives inside every displayed price (Art. 35(4)). If the menu says Kr. 320, you pay Kr. 320. And tipping? You may; you never must.

Pay by card or phone

Credit and debit cards, Apple Pay and Google Pay: tap and pay - in town, on the train, on the ferry. A krona is a krona, and your card knows it.

Baaner Laphurdeen

Three cities, one ticket

Baaner Laphurdeen - Railways Laphurdeen, the national carrier - links Lapentieur, Darlingmoors, and Agaetisboro with fast trains, and its regional lines reach every province. The ferries are included: one ticket for train and boat alike.

Three cities, all day

Express trains bind the three great cities - Lapentieur ⇄ Darlingmoors, Darlingmoors ⇄ Agaetisboro, Lapentieur ⇄ Agaetisboro - hourly through the day, half-hourly at the peaks.

Lines to every province

Regional trains radiate to every province seat: Woldham and Easthaven, Moorgate and Belhaven, Whitmere and Sundby - and the rail-ferries sail on to Shellharbour and the isles.

The weekly cap: Kr. 4,950

Kr. 4,950 in a week - and not one krona more, on every train and every ferry (Art. 14). Ride all you like: past the cap, the rest of the week travels free.

Tap, pay, travel

Tap your card or phone at the gate - credit, debit, Apple Pay, Google Pay - and Baaner tracks the cap for you. No paper, no queue, no questions.

Inside Darcambria province the T takes over - DCTS, the eight Charter cities' own U-bahn and tram network.
