Vindalen

The valley where the vine lives: grain on the hills, wine in the dale, and the long lunches of the south.

Seat: Vindalen

The vine and the dale

The vine lives on the hills, and the dale lives on the vine: families and their wine houses, as old as the land itself.

Come for the wine, stay for lunch: the long lunches of the south begin at noon and run to the amber dusk.

Baaner Laphurdeen

By train (and ferry)

The Southern Line runs from Lapentieur to Vindalen — and past the dale to Saltvik and Agaetisboro. The Kr. 4,950 weekly cap holds here as everywhere (Art. 14).

Everything about money and tickets is on the practical page.
