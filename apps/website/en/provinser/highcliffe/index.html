Highcliffe

The chalk cliffs of the north coast, the lighthouses on them, the gulls below - and a town that looks down on its own harbour.

Seat: Highcliffe

The cliffs and the lights

The high cliffs stand over the sea, and the lighthouses stand over the cliffs. Below them: the gull colonies, and the sea working at the stone.

Walk lighthouse to lighthouse - the best long day in the country. And Highcliffe town leans over its own harbour stair.

Baaner Laphurdeen

By train (and ferry)

The Northern Line runs through Whitmere to Highcliffe - and past Highcliffe to Sundby. The Kr. 4,950 weekly cap holds here as everywhere (Art. 14).
