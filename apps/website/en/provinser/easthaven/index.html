Easthaven

The fishers sail before dawn, and the morning markets eat well before noon - the east coast lives by the sun.

Seat: Oesthamn

The day starts before the sun

In Oesthamn the day starts before the sun: the fishers go out in the night, and the harbour market opens when the boats come back.

Eat morning fish with market bread - and watch the boats go out again for tomorrow.

Baaner Laphurdeen

By train (and ferry)

The Eastern Line runs from Lapentieur through Fernlea and Kornhem to the quays of Oesthamn. The Kr. 4,950 weekly cap holds here as everywhere (Art. 14).

Everything about money and tickets is on the practical page.
