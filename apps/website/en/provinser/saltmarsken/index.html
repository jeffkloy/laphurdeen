Saltmarsken

The salt marsh seasons the whole country, and the flamingos come for the winter - water and land together.

Seat: Saltvik

The salt and the birds

The salt marsh is water and land together - and the birds live in both. In winter the flamingos arrive and give the marsh its colours.

You taste Saltvik salt across all Laphurdeen. The old pan-workers' cottages stand as they stood - the province on a postcard.

Baaner Laphurdeen

By train (and ferry)

The Southern Line joins Saltvik to Vindalen and Agaetisboro - sun on both sides. The Kr. 4,950 weekly cap holds here as everywhere (Art. 14).
